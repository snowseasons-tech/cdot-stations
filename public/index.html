Colorado Inspection Stations

CSP/CDOT commercial vehicle ports of entry · status is reported manually, not live from the state

North American Standard inspections at the fixed port, from FMCSA's public Vehicle Inspection File. Roadside patrol stops in the same town are excluded. Vehicle OOS rate is the share of vehicle inspections (Levels I, II, V) with a vehicle out-of-service violation; driver OOS rate likewise over driver inspections (Levels I, II, III).
