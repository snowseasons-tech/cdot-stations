# Installing the CDOT Stations app

For team members. It takes about a minute, and you only do it once. The app opens like any other app on your phone, and you stay signed in for a while between uses.

**Address:** `https://stations.snowseasonstech.net` (your team lead will send you this link)

## iPhone

1. Open the link in **Safari**.
2. Sign in: enter the email address your team lead added, then type the 6-digit code Cloudflare emails you.
3. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up, at the bottom of the screen).
4. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then tap **Add**.
5. Open **CDOT Stations** from your Home Screen. (Use the new icon from now on, not Safari.)

The page also shows these steps at the top the first time you open it in Safari. Tap **Not now** to hide them.

## Android

1. Open the link in **Chrome**.
2. Sign in with your email and the code Cloudflare sends you.
3. Tap **Install** on the card at the top of the page, or open the Chrome menu (three dots) and choose **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).
4. Open **CDOT Stations** from your home screen or app drawer.

## What you can do in the app

You can see every station's status and note, the inspection numbers, and the map of where inspections happened. **The app is read-only for now**: there are no Update buttons. Statuses are updated from the web page on a computer.

## Get notified when a station changes

1. Open the installed app (from your Home Screen icon, not the browser).
2. Tap **Follow** on each station you care about, or turn on **Notify me about every station**.
3. When asked, tap **Allow** to let the app send notifications.

When someone changes a followed station's status or note, you'll get a notification like "Limon: Open: No wait". Tap it to jump to that station. Tap **Following** again to stop.

On iPhone this works only in the app you added to the Home Screen (not in Safari), and needs **iOS 16.4 or newer**. If the panel and Follow buttons don't appear, the app may not be installed yet, or notifications may not be set up on the server yet.

## If something is off

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Your sign-in has expired." | Tap **Sign in again**, enter your email and the new code. This happens now and then, by design. |
| "Can't reach the server. Showing the last data." | You have no signal, or the server is down. Tap **Retry** when you have a connection. |
| No code arrives | Check spam, and make sure you used the exact email your team lead added. Anyone not on the list is turned away. |
| No **Add to Home Screen** on iPhone | Open the link in **Safari** (not inside another app, such as Mail or Messages). |
| "Notifications are blocked" | Turn them back on for the app in your phone's settings (on iPhone: Settings, then scroll to the app), then reopen it. |
| Notifications stop arriving | Open the app, tap **Following** to unfollow and **Follow** again on the stations you want. |
| The page looks old | Close the app fully and reopen it; it checks for updates each time. |

To remove the app, press and hold its icon and choose **Remove App** (iPhone) or **Uninstall** (Android).
