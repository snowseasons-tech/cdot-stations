Colorado Inspection Stations

CSP/CDOT commercial vehicle ports of entry · status is reported manually, not live from the state

Install this app
  1. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow) in Safari.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.
  3. Open CDOT Stations from your Home Screen.

Notifications, when they arrive, will need iOS 16.4 or newer.

Live traffic, closures, construction & weather

Real-time road conditions come from CDOT, not from this app. Open them at the source:

  • COtrip.org: CDOT's live map of traffic, road closures, construction, weather and cameras.
  • COtrip Planner app: iPhone · Android
  • CDOT on X (@ColoradoDOT): road and travel updates.

Each station below also has a "Live road conditions nearby" link that opens COtrip on that spot.

Inspection levels

North American Standard inspections at the fixed port, from FMCSA's public Vehicle Inspection File. Roadside patrol stops in the same town are excluded. Vehicle OOS rate is the share of vehicle inspections (Levels I, II, V) with a vehicle out-of-service violation; driver OOS rate likewise over driver inspections (Levels I, II, III).

Search inspections

    Fixed-port inspections in Colorado from FMCSA's public Vehicle Inspection File, newest first, looked up live when you search. Roadside patrol stops are excluded. Carrier names are as FMCSA publishes them. Download CSV saves the results shown so far; use Load more first to include more.
